## What’s new

- **An upgrade now waits for your agents.** They are asked to hand off and confirm before anything restarts, with a modal showing the upgrade beside every agent’s status. Force Restart skips the wait.
- **Your agents stop losing mail.** A message was marked delivered when its bytes reached a pipe, not when the agent received it — so inbox mail could be consumed unseen and never arrive.
- **One update control, on the Genie label.** It shows the version you are running, or “Upgrade to …” when there is one. The duplicate banner is gone.
- **Editing an agent’s prompt creates its `AGENT.md`** instead of refusing to save and telling you to re-register the agent.
- **Mail from a workspace is tagged as that workspace’s**, so a shared Mailpit can tell whose message is whose.
- **A security patch that reaches you**: the update feed’s YAML parser could be made to burn CPU. Two Next.js CVEs were also patched; neither ships in Genie.

## Migration

- Nothing to do. Your next upgrade will pause to ask your agents rather than restarting under them.
