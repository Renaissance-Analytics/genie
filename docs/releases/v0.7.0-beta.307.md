## What’s new

- **An Escape no longer jams your agent’s mail.** Escape and the arrow keys cost Genie its certainty that your box was empty, so inbox messages parked instead of arriving and the Send button refused.
- **When Genie cannot tell, you can settle it.** A parked notice now offers “Clear input & send” — a TUI that empties its own composer is invisible to Genie, and that button is the way through.
- **A cycled completion no longer gets typed over.** Shift-Tab was read as the terminal talking back rather than as you typing, leaving Genie sure a full box was empty.
- **AgentInbox is how you reach another agent**, and agents are told so — no more messages sent down channels the other agent never reads.
- **A workspace can declare a Postgres extension**, and Genie installs it into the database it manages, surviving a rebuild.
- **`connectToGenie` says which terminal is missing** rather than reporting the whole workspace as unknown.
- **A narrower sidebar**, giving the width back to your terminals.

## Migration

- Nothing to do. Genie must restart to pick this up, and a restart clears any jammed draft on its own.
