# Genie 2.0.0-beta.67

**Genie now has its own launcher. It starts Genie, keeps it up to date part by part, and your agents keep working while Genie moves onto it. This release also fixes account switching stopping a busy agent.**

## What's new

### The Genie launcher
- **Genie moves onto its new launcher by itself.** The first time this update installs, a card reads "Moving Genie to its new launcher… This takes a few seconds, and your agents keep working." Your agents, terminals and settings carry over, and "Start on logon" and "Start minimised" stay as you set them.
- **A new tray panel.** Click Genie's tray icon to see your host and how many agents are working, and to open Genie, open the Genie Browser, stop or start the host, see updates, choose whether Genie starts when you sign in, close Genie (your agents keep running) or shut everything down.
- **A launch pad.** Click the top of the tray panel to see your host, Genie and the Genie Browser in one place, each with its own Open or Stop button, and the updates that are ready.
- **Each part of Genie updates on its own.** The Updates window lists the host, the Genie client, the Genie Browser and the launcher separately. A client or Browser update never restarts your host. A host update pauses your agents briefly, then puts each one back.
- **Your tools are in Updates too.** Node, PHP and the other tools your host manages appear beside Genie's own parts, and Update installs them through your host. With no host running, they say "Start the host to check its tools."
- **A failed host update rolls itself back.** If a new host doesn't start, Genie goes back to the previous one and your agents return.
- **The Genie Browser opens from the tray**, beside your Genie window, with the window closed, or after the host restarts.
- **Genie can open as a window only.** Pick a host from the list, or add one, without running a host on this computer.
- **No more console windows.** Genie starts from its shortcut, at logon and after an install without a console window, and its icon shows on the shortcut.
- **Every update is checked before it runs.** Each part of Genie is signed; if a download is not Genie's, nothing is installed and Genie says so.

### Accounts and limits
- **Switching accounts no longer stops a busy agent.** An agent finishes its turn before it moves. Its header, chat and Dashboard row say "On <old> · moves to <new> at next idle", and **Restart on <new> now** moves it straight away.
- **A switched-to account's usage shows at once.** Genie reads it as soon as you switch, the first time an account is used, and while the Accounts panel is open. Every account also has a **Check usage now** button.
- **An agent that can't move says so.** It reads "Stays on <old>" with the reason; restarting it there asks you first, because it starts a new conversation.
- **The limit line names its account**, for example "67% of 7d window left on Aux".
- **An agent that hits its limit is held, not lost.** Genie says so where it waits, and if another account has room, one click switches to it. A hold that resumes on another day shows the day.
- **Messages sent during a hold or a move are kept.** They show "Waiting · sent when <agent> is back" and are delivered once the agent is up again. **Send all** delivers every waiting message and shows each result.
- **Choose what happens when the active account runs out:** Settings → Providers → "When the active account runs out": **Ask me** (default) or **Switch to the account with the most room**.

### Questions
- **A question window no longer closes when its agent restarts.** Restarts, account moves and upgrades leave it open, and your answer still reaches the agent. If the agent is removed, the window keeps your draft and offers **Answer anyway**.

## Known limitations
- Some launcher screens don't yet look exactly as designed: there is no separate "Starting" screen yet, and the install, ready, update, error and rollback screens differ in small details. They work; they'll be brought in line in a later release.
- **Update all** and the tray's update count cover Genie's own parts, not your tools; update tools one by one.
- A tool update that needs your password may refuse with a message instead of asking for it.
- Checking an account's usage sends one tiny message from that account. On its own, Genie reads each account at most once every 15 minutes.
- A slow but normal "Shut down everything" can still be recorded as an unclean end and start Genie in safe mode next time. **Leave safe mode** gets you out in one click.
