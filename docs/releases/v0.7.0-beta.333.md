## What’s new

- **The question modal stays on screen and can be resized.** A ForceTheQuestion window that opened off the display is moved back onto it, so its action buttons are always reachable.
- **Right-clicking an agent opens the agent's menu.** A running agent used to get the terminal menu, so Delete killed its terminal and only a second attempt offered to delete the agent.
- **The panel × puts the panel away instead of killing the terminal.** The session keeps running and comes back when you open it again; stopping it is still offered where it says so.
- **A sidecar no longer appears as a second agent.** It is reached by flipping its driver's panel, and only shows a square of its own if its driver is gone.
- **A sleeping workspace takes its panels with it.** Hibernated workspaces stop listing their terminals under Unattached.
- **An idle agent stays reachable after you touch its prompt.** AgentInbox mail could be suppressed indefinitely once a human typed at a terminal that never took another turn.
