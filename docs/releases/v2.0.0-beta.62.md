# Genie 2.0.0-beta.62


**Genie stops crashing: the window no longer runs out of memory. Plus the phone, Stop, upgrades, drones and much more. Please update every machine.**


## What's new

- **No more window crashes.** Genie's window could grow to several GB and freeze or vanish within minutes on a busy machine. Two causes are fixed: the window sent every agent's full history to Genie's background service many times a second, and the page queued a full copy of your workspace on every update. In a 30-minute test shaped like a busy desktop (32 agents, 240 open questions, thousands of messages) the window now stays around 300 MB.
- **A broken panel no longer blanks the window.** If one part of the screen fails (a tab strip, the rail, an approval card), that part shows an error with Retry and the rest keeps working.
- **Stop always stops.** Stop ends a turn within seconds, even when the agent is silent or held by a rate limit; your queued messages are kept, and a rate-limited turn says so.
- **Upgrades wait for agents.** An upgrade or quit lets running agents finish their turn (up to 2 minutes, with "Upgrade now" to skip), then brings every agent back once.
- **The phone is usable.** Swipe between the agent list, its chat and its work stream; answer every kind of question; attach files and camera photos (photos from the phone now reach the agent).
- **Handoffs no longer pile up for you.** An agent's handoff is kept for agents; it never shows as something you must read or clear.
- **Switching chats shows the whole conversation at once,** never mail cards alone while the replies load.
- **Commands agents run are visible.** A command an agent runs through Genie shows in the workspace's Processes with live output (secrets masked) and Stop, and the chat links to the exact run.
- **Drones:** they reach the Tynn relay again; a Pilot names a drone on an older Genie and reconnects by itself after the drone updates.
- **Do Not Disturb is a bell in the header,** obvious when it's on.
- **Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line;** the hint sits in the composer; sent images show as thumbnails.
- **Also:** the chat stays on the agent you picked; Remote access settings show only this machine's own; question options are full-width cards; background processes set to "never restart" stay stopped after Genie restarts; the sites and services tools work with no window open; a broken Claude/Codex install is repaired with the vendor's installer from Genie.

## Known limitations

- **A terminal can stop redrawing** until you scroll or resize it.
- **Raw "unhandled promise rejection" items can still appear in the Deck** while Genie restarts (the fix is in progress for beta.63).
- **The window still does more work than it should with very many open questions or mails** (it peaks near 0.9 GB in the heaviest test); a follow-up is planned.

## Not in this build

- Plain-words errors instead of raw rejection items in the Deck, and retrying cut-off calls (#636, in progress).
- The window/relay status lag (#656), Tailscale sign-in "link expired" (#708).
- Several accounts per provider (63), Genie Mail (64), Compliance (65).
- The remaining host-split phases, ADF, GApps V8/V9, the sound picker, TRUECOST, CI in Flows, missed scheduled runs, the approval UI redesign.
- macOS.
