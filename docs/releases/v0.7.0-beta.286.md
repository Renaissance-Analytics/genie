## What’s new

- Your agents show the real logo of the TUI running them. Claude was wearing Tynn’s mark, and Genie’s own TUI was too.
- Switch the TUI an agent runs under from the panel’s own controls. The driver you switch away from keeps its conversation as a sidecar you can flip back to — switching never stops anything.
- Agent settings now describes the AGENT: its name, its drivers and sidecars, and whether it is this workspace’s default. It used to be titled after the TUI.
- Every workspace can designate a **default agent** in its settings — the one that boots from the workspace root. It replaces the empty “workspace” agent that appeared in every sidebar and did nothing when clicked.
- Workspaces have an avatar instead of a generic cube: their initials by default, and an icon you can set.
- Hovering the agents on a workspace row peeks at who is working there, which TUI each is on, and whether any sidecars are live.
- The Genie OS agent can be restarted again, and now starts with the tools and skills its own instructions tell it to use — it had none.
- Genie reconnects an agent to its tools before telling it about an upgrade, so the first thing it reads is not a broken tool call.
- The AgentInbox channel connects again. It was announcing itself in a way no client could read.
- A GApp Development Workspace keeps its distinct look while its row is pulsing.

## Migration

- No action is required. Your agents, conversations and inboxes are preserved.
- The empty “workspace” agent is removed from every workspace. Genie does not choose a replacement — pick your default agent in workspace settings when you want one, and until then a workspace simply has none.
- Restarting an agent, switching its TUI, or leaving a sidecar running never stops anything on its own. Stopping a TUI stays something you do deliberately.
