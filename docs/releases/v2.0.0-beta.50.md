# Genie 2.0.0-beta.50

## What's new

### Connect to your other workstations
- **A Connect button in the title bar,** as in Genie 1. It lists your workstations from Tynn, each with Open, and refreshes every time you open it.
- **⌘K → "Connect to my workstations"** and **"Open a share link…"**.
- **Each Open shows its progress,** then either success or the reason it failed with Retry.
- **After you switch Tynn accounts,** Open always connects as the account you're signed in with now.

### Pilots and Drones
- **Pilot:** your first desktop. It can browse and connect to your other workstations.
- **Drone:** every other machine, including all cloud workstations. A Drone never connects out and never opens share links. It accepts only you (from your own paired machines) and guests who came through a share link.
- **Changing a machine's role** (Remote access → a workstation → Make pilot / Make drone) needs your Tynn authenticator code. Tynn enforces it, so nothing on the machine itself can change it.
- **One machine, one workstation:** reinstalling or wiping Genie no longer creates a duplicate.

### Tynn tab
- **Your workspace's Tynn board in Genie:** drag cards between columns, and create, edit and view stories and tasks.

### Fixes
- **Toolchain:** every Update button works. PHP 8.4.27 (the security fix) installs through Genie's own installer, and progress, failure and Retry are visible.
- **Flows:** opening a flow opens the canvas editor, not a second Genie window.
- **Question window:** the drag strip stays put, and each new question starts at the top.
- **Chat:**
  - A message that fails to send stays in the box with the reason.
  - Chat history is no longer silently lost when its file is busy.
- **Agents:** an agent's own permission switches answer its approvals ("May drive pages", "May manage workspace tabs").
- **Folders:** one folder is one workspace.

### Also
- **Each workspace has its own colour,** so you can tell agents apart. On first launch Genie writes it into each workspace's `project.json`, so each envelope shows one changed file.
- **Files → Export…** on a folder saves it as a .zip.

## Known limitations

- **macOS** isn't supported right now, and no macOS builds are published.
- **A Drone's own screen** can take up to 5 minutes to show a role change made elsewhere. Tynn enforces the change immediately, and a refused connection re-reads the role at once.
- **The Tynn tab** covers the board only. Claiming and assigning, live updates and the other Tynn pages come in later releases.
- **This machine's own role** can't be changed from this machine. Use another Pilot, or tynn.ai.

## How it was verified

- **CI:** every change was merged with its tests failing first, then green. Main CI was green on every job, including Windows unit and packaging.
- **Two-VM proof:** a Linux and a Windows test VM ran at the same time against a simulated Tynn and relay.
  1. A Pilot connected to the other machine.
  2. A guest opened a share link to a Drone.
  3. A Drone was refused when it tried to connect out.
  4. A role change with a real authenticator code took effect, and the Drone lost its connection in the same second.

  The connect-flow fixes were then proved again on both VMs.
- **Toolchain:** a real PHP 8.4.24 → 8.4.27 update ran on the Windows VM, and node, go and python updates ran on the Linux VM.
