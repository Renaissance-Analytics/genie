## What’s new

- **Sleeping workspaces stay out of the way.** Hibernated workspaces are hidden by default, with a counted control to reveal them in their original order.
- **Older installs upgrade without hanging at sign-in.** Genie repairs the obsolete workspace database constraint before migration and preserves every row, column and index.
- **Shared sessions honor their grants.** Guest access is limited to its allowed workspaces and capabilities, with unclassified host routes denied by default.
- **Questions and user tasks go to the right place.** Agents use ForceTheQuestion for answers and decisions, and reserve UserList for actions only a person can take.
