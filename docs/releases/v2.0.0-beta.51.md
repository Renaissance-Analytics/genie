# Genie 2.0.0-beta.51

## What's new

### Workstations, cleaned up
- **The list no longer shows the machine you're on.** Machines that Genie 1 and Genie 2 both registered no longer appear twice.
- **One line per machine:** full name, a small Pilot/Drone badge, Open, and a dot showing whether the machine is **online** (filled), **offline** (hollow) or **unknown** (dashed). Hover a dot to see which.
- **Connecting shows the machine's name** and each step: asking Tynn, reaching the machine, opening its Genie. An offline machine gets a plain sentence with Retry, with no raw ids and no Host/PIN form.
- **Genie 1 records carry over.** When you sign in, Genie 2 takes over the record Genie 1 made for the same machine instead of creating a second one. Shares on it are kept, and any duplicate is retired.
- **Pilot/Drone switching** has moved to Settings → Remote access.

### Talking to agents
- **Genie Dashboard:** clicking an agent switches the docked chat to it and keeps the Dashboard on screen. This now also works when the agent is waiting on something; before, it jumped to the Deck.
- **A workspace's dashboard:** clicking an agent opens its tab (activity and chat), and the docked chat follows.

### Tynn tab
- **Claim, unclaim and assign** stories and tasks, with the claimant's initials on each card.
- **Every edit field tynn.ai has,** including tags, persona, outcome, risk, effort and definition of done on stories, and environment, blocked-by and produces on tasks.
- **Five board icons that showed blank** now show.

## Known limitations

- **macOS** isn't supported right now, and no macOS builds are published.
- **Online dots** show "unknown" until the relay update that reports presence is live.
- **The Tynn tab** covers the board only. Live updates, versions and the other Tynn pages come later.

## How it was verified

- **Tests first:** every change merged with its tests failing first, then passing. Main CI was green on every job, including Windows unit and packaging. The full suite passed on the Linux test VM.
- **Two-VM proof:**
  - this machine is hidden from its own list;
  - the redesigned connecting window works, including the offline sentence;
  - a machine goes online, then offline about 10 s after its Genie stops;
  - a Genie 1 record is taken over on sign-in with its share intact and the duplicate retired.
- **Dashboard click:** a real-window check clicks a waiting agent on the Dashboard and stays put. Two more do the same on a workspace dashboard, by click and by Enter.
