## What’s new

- **Genie 2: the Deck is what opens.** Everything waiting on you, every agent, and what happened while you were away — instead of four transcripts you could not read.
- **The grid is still here**, just not what opens. Reach it with ⌘K → “The grid (all workspaces)”. Your workspaces, panels, layouts and agents are untouched.
- **Agents have their own screen** — conversation, plan, tool calls and approvals, with the terminal one tab away and still live, so taking over is instant.
- **Open a terminal, or an agent in TUI mode, in its own window** from any workspace, so the main window stays clean while something long-running works.
- **⌘K / Ctrl+K opens anywhere.** It used to need a focused terminal, and it now finds Hosts, Sharing, Knowledge, Flows and the App Store, not just workspaces and prompts.
- **Two title-bar icons are gone.** Questions collect on the Deck, processes live in the workspace process manager, and both are in ⌘K.
- **An agent’s question no longer steals your keyboard**, Settings stops hiding behind the main window, and a private agent stays repliable instead of decaying mid-chat.

## Migration

- Nothing to do, but the first thing you see changed: Genie opens on the Deck. Nothing was removed, and the grid is one ⌘K away. Agents still run in a pty unless you opt in to ACP.
