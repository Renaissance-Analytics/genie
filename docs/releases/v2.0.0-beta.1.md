## What's new

- **Genie 2: the Deck is what opens.** Everything waiting on you, every agent, and what happened while you were away — instead of four transcripts you could not read.
- **Agents have their own screen.** Conversation, plan, tool calls and approvals, with the terminal one tab away and still live. Sending and approving needs ACP switched on.
- **First run is two steps.** Pick a folder; Genie checks the one driver it needs and starts an agent with a prompt ready. No sign-in wall, no GitHub, no toolchain gate.
- **Tynn is optional.** Everything local works signed out. Tynn-backed features say what they need instead of holding the whole app behind a sign-in.
- **The title bar is empty — eight icons gone, nothing lost.** Every feature is in ⌘K, and the signals those icons carried now sit on the Deck, where you are looking.
- **⌘K opens anywhere, including over an open panel**, where a dimmer used to swallow the click. It finds Hosts, Sharing, Knowledge, Flows and the App Store too.
- **The grid is still here**, just not what opens — ⌘K → "The grid (all workspaces)". Your workspaces, panels, layouts and agents are untouched.

## Migration

- Nothing to do, but the first thing you see changed: Genie opens on the Deck, and the grid is one ⌘K away. Agents still run in a pty unless you opt in to ACP.
