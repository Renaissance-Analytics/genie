# Genie launcher 2026.11.28-1

## What's new
- **Genie's own launcher.** It installs, starts and updates Genie's host, client and Browser, each from its own folder, and checks every part's signature before it runs it.
- **The tray panel.** Click Genie's tray icon to see your host and its agents, open Genie or the Genie Browser, stop or start the host, see updates, choose whether Genie starts when you sign in, close Genie (agents keep running) or shut everything down.
- **The launch pad.** Your host, Genie and the Genie Browser in one place, each with its own Open or Stop button, and the updates that are ready.
- **Updates, part by part.** The Updates window lists the host, the client, the Browser, the launcher and your host's tools. A client or Browser update never restarts your host, and a host update that fails to start rolls itself back.
- **Starts with your computer** when you choose, and moves an existing Genie onto itself with your agents still running.
