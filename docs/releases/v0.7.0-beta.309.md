## What’s new

- **A terminal is told when it predates one of your services.** A terminal only gets connections that existed when it opened, so one started before Mailpit never saw it, and nothing said so.
- **That is reported separately from a stale terminal**, because they mean different things: one is missing a service, the other is dialling one that moved. Missing no longer reads as broken.
- **Services are named, not their variables.** “This terminal predates Mailpit” rather than three environment keys you have to recognise.

## Migration

- Nothing to do. Your app is unaffected either way — Genie keeps the repo’s `.env` current. Reopen a terminal Genie names to give it the connections it missed.
