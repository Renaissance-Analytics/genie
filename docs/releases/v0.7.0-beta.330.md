## What’s new

- **Desktop Genies can be reached over Tynn.** Authorized guests can connect to workstation terminals and sites through the shared relay without exposing loopback services.
- **Hosts can see and remove each guest.** The master window shows who is connected and what they can reach, with a per-session disconnect action.
- **Terminal sizing recovers after remote viewers leave.** When the final remote detaches, the local terminal refits to the desktop instead of remaining oversized.
- **Light mode is readable everywhere.** Knowledge Graph, Docs, Testing Browser, master menus, notices and native window chrome now follow the active theme.
