# Genie host 2026.11.28-1

## What's new
- **Your host runs under the launcher.** It keeps running when you close the Genie window, and a failed host update goes back to the previous host and restores your agents.
- **Your tools are checked and installed by your host** from the launcher's Updates.
- **An agent that hits its account's limit is held**, and when another account has room Genie can move it there: you choose **Ask me** or **Switch to the account with the most room** in Settings → Providers.
- **The Genie Browser opens even when no window is open.**
