## What’s new

- **Your panels stay how you left them.** A remote connect no longer reopens every panel you closed, and it honours your Max views setting instead of opening one per terminal the host has.
- **The workspace sidebar starts collapsed** instead of opening every workspace expanded.
- **Pairing stops forgetting your devices.** A key it could not read once made Genie mint a new PIN over your paired devices; the old one is now kept, and Genie says what went wrong.
- **Force Restart no longer loses your agents.** Skipping the handoff used to skip recording what was running, so nothing came back afterwards.
- **Lists, per agent and per workspace.** An agent keeps a short list that rides its “I’m done”; ticking an item off your list nudges the agent that wrote it.

## Migration

- Nothing to do. Your sidebar will open collapsed once — expand what you want and it is remembered.
